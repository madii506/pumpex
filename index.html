01 — How it works

Every coin is a well.
Every trade fills it.

You launch a coin on pump.fun through pumpex. It is born with pump.fun's holder rewards switched on, so its creator is the coin's own holder-rewards account, not a person. Every trade pays a creator fee into the well, and the well pays it out to the people holding the coin.

05 — Docs

The plain version.

Where do the fees go?

Every coin launched through pumpex is created with pump.fun's is_holder_reward flag on. That sets the coin's creator to its own holder-rewards PDA, so creator fees accrue to that PDA's creator vault and are paid out to holders through pump.fun's distribute_fee_to_holders. Nobody, including us, can withdraw them.

What does “pair with any meme” mean?

pump.fun's create_v2 accepts a quote mint. With a pump.fun meme as the quote, your coin's bonding curve is priced in that meme, and every buy of your coin goes through it. pump.fun limits how deep these chains go and rejects memes that are not eligible; pumpex checks this live before you sign.

Does pumpex hold any keys or funds?

No. The transaction is built by pumpex's server from pump.fun's official SDK, signed by the coin's fresh mint key in your browser, then signed and sent by your own wallet. pumpex never sees a private key and never receives a fee.

What does it cost?

The pump.fun account rent for a new coin plus the Solana network fee, paid from your wallet. pumpex adds nothing.

Where is $PUMPEX in this?

$PUMPEX is well #000, the launchpad's own coin. Its address appears here once it is live. Next on the engine: fees from wells buying $PUMPEX for holders.

Can I verify everything?

Yes. Every launch links to its transaction on Solscan and its page on pump.fun. The creator of every pumpex coin is its holder-rewards PDA, which you can check on the coin's bonding curve account.
